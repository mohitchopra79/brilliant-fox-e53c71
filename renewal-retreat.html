I · The Journey

Two hidden worlds of Costa Rica, one unhurried path back to yourself.

Your retreat begins deep in a volcanic canyon, where a mineral-rich thermal river winds through a tropical forest. This spectacular 1,500-acre private reserve in Guanacaste of the Río Perdido is our starting point on a journey of reconnection back to yourself and others. Soak in hot springs beneath 160-foot trees, enjoy a nourishing volcanic mud bath at the river's edge, and restore with movement sessions in a private jungle Shala witnessed by lush forest backdrops. Through a thoughtfully curated program of somatic practices, guided inquiry, and mindful movement, feel inspired through renewed perspectives on peace, resilience, and wellbeing on this transformational journey in the secluded heart of a volcanic sierra region.

Midway through the journey, The Renewal Retreat turns west where the jungle meets the Pacific ocean. Our next location shifts from thermal river reflections to barefoot ocean connections on the beaches of Santa Teresa on the Nicoya Peninsula. Home to one of the world's five famous Blue Zones, we'll settle into beachfront luxury at Hotel Nantipa and embrace sunrise movement practices in the forest gardens, delight in sea-to-table dinners at Manzú, and spend our evenings relaxing with the Pacific sunsets this coast amply offers. A perfect location to embody the natural rhythms that Costa Rica's legendary Pura Vida is known for.

The Renewal Retreat offers a transformational journey of daily activities with nature experiences and a wellness program focused on nurturing a connection with the innate wisdom of the body. When the body is placed in a supportive environment, the mind experiences peace and calm. Experience daily somatic movement, restorative yoga classes and workshops on resilience, inner parts work and self-compassion. The in-between moments are yours for spa rituals, waterfalls, horseback rides and other activities, or simply, the hammock.

Tara is a trauma-informed Somatic Practitioner, Restorative Yoga Teacher, and Environmental Educator based in Vancouver, B.C. Canada. With more than fifteen years in wellness, holistic health, education and travel experience, her work draws together somatic movement practices with certifications in somatic attachment and integrative somatic trauma therapy, yin and restorative yoga, herbal medicine and mindfulness practices to guide and inspire transformational journeys.

Costa Rica is familiar ground for Tara. She completed six-month Master's fieldwork in Environmental and Holistic Health Studies. Tara draws upon the natural environment as an additional resource for attunement, reflection, and for embodied awareness that guests take home with them making these idyllic locations in Costa Rica the perfect experience for The Renewal Retreat.

The canyon

Nights 1 – 4 · 16 to 20 July 2027

Río Perdido

The Lost River · San Bernardo canyonlands, Guanacaste

Set on a 1,500-acre private reserve where the cool Río Blanco meets the hot mineral currents of Río Perdido, The Renewal Retreat begins at a gathering place once sacred to the Chorotega people of Costa Rica. Its founders pledged to never develop more than one per cent of the land, and have lived to this promise until this day. We will begin our experience with 4-night accommodation in the heart of this volcanic sierra. Our companions include capuchin and howler monkeys who are regular visitors along the trails, and where the magic of the thermal river flows through the land.

Living well, living long

The Blue Zone of Nicoya

Santa Teresa lies on the Nicoya Peninsula. As one of only five places on earth identified as a Blue Zone, people live measurably longer, healthier lives.

The term “Blue Zone” comes from longevity research led by the National Geographic explorer Dan Buettner with demographers, who mapped global regions where unusually many people reach their nineties and beyond. On the Nicoya Peninsula, they found villages with a significantly higher rate of longevity than the rest of Costa Rica, and a surprisingly higher number of centenarians.

  • Okinawa, Japan
  • Sardinia, Italy
  • Ikaria, Greece
  • Loma Linda, California
  • Nicoya, Costa Rica
  1. i.

    Plan de vida

    Nicoyans speak of a “life plan:” a reason to rise each morning and a sense of purpose carried well into old age.

  2. ii.

    Family close by

    Several generations live under one roof and have close community ties, with elders at the heart of family life.

  3. iii.

    Natural movement

    Life is lived outdoors — walking, tending the land, and working with the hands rather than structured exercise.

  4. iv.

    The traditional table

    Healthy food with black beans daily, corn tortillas, squash, plantains, papaya and fresh fish with gallo pinto at breakfast.

  5. v.

    Mineral-rich water

    The peninsula's water is naturally high in calcium, believed to help keep bones strong.

  6. vi.

    Light evenings, long sun

    A light, early dinner and outdoor living throughout the year.

How the Blue Zone shapes our days on The Renewal Retreat

  • Mornings begin outdoors, and with somatic movement like the Nicoyan spirit of natural, daily movement.
  • Workshops on resiliency, Inner Parts & self-compassion invite reflection and purpose — your own plan de vida.
  • Nantipa's wellness philosophy is built on Blue Zone living, and Manzú's kitchen celebrates Nicoyan ingredients including its Rondón stew of coconut and seafood.
  • Shared meals around one table with wholesome food — the most enduring Blue Zone ritual of all.
