Into the Indian jungle

Three-quarters of the world's wild tigers. The last wild lions outside Africa. Rhino on the Brahmaputra floodplain and snow leopard at four thousand metres — inside one country, with a palace and a Taj Mahal on the way home.

If you have already done Africa

The same thrill.
A different animal.

A Serengeti migration and a Kruger game drive are extraordinary, and most of our wildlife travellers have already done both. India is not a substitute — it is a different proposition. You track one animal rather than scan for many, in forest rather than open plain, and you find it in a country whose temples, palaces and food would justify the flight on their own.

No other country puts five apex species within two weeks of travel.
