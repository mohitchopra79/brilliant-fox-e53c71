Itinerary Themes

Or begin with a theme.

The Golden Triangle

Delhi, Agra and Jaipur for first-time travellers. Six to ten days of monuments, marigolds and an unforgettable dawn at the Taj.

Royal Rajasthan

Two to three weeks through Jaipur, Jodhpur, Udaipur, Jaisalmer — heritage havelis, lake palaces, camel dunes at Khuri.

Kerala & the South

Backwater houseboats, Cochin spice markets, hill-station tea plantations, Ayurvedic retreats on the Malabar coast.

The Himalayan North

Ladakh's monasteries, Shimla's colonial bones, the toy train to Darjeeling, Bhutan as an extension if time allows.

Wildlife & Wilderness

Tigers at Ranthambore and Bandhavgarh, leopards at Jhalana, the one-horned rhinoceros at Kaziranga, jungle lodges throughout.

Festivals & Fairs

Holi in Mathura, Diwali in Jaipur, the Pushkar camel fair, Onam in Kerala, the Hornbill Festival in Nagaland — timed and built around the date.

Two Layers Worth Adding

Add a forest. Or a week of stillness.

Two things travellers rarely think to ask for, and almost always thank us for afterwards — a few nights in a tiger reserve, and an unhurried stay at Ananda in the Himalayas. Neither needs its own holiday. Both fold into a private journey without pulling it apart.

Add a Wilderness
2 – 7 nights · Slots into any route

A jungle, folded into the drive

India holds roughly three-quarters of the world's wild tigers, the only Asiatic lions left on earth, and the one-horned rhino of the Brahmaputra floodplain. Two drives a day, an open 4×4, a naturalist assigned to you, and a lodge of eight to twenty-five suites rather than sixty rooms.

Ranthambore · 2 – 3 nights · Oct – Jun

Three hours from Jaipur, so the simplest tiger reserve to drop into a Golden Triangle or Rajasthan itinerary. Tented camps at the park gate.

Bandhavgarh & Kanha · 6 – 7 nights · Oct – Jun

The serious version — the highest tiger density in India, then Kipling's sal forest and the barasingha. Flown into via Jabalpur, usually after Agra.

Kaziranga · 3 – 4 nights · Nov – Apr

Assam, for one-horned rhino at close quarters — sightings are near certain, often within the first hour. Pairs with Kolkata or a Himalayan leg.

Gir · 2 – 3 nights · Dec – Apr

The last wild Asiatic lions, in Gujarat, and remarkably easy to find. An easy addition to a Mumbai or Rajasthan route.

Kabini · 3 nights · Oct – May

For journeys in the south — elephant herds and the black panther on the Kabini backwaters, two hours from Mysuru.

Add a Stillness
5 – 14 nights · Narendra Nagar, Uttarakhand

A week at Ananda, mid‑journey

A hundred acres of sal forest and a Maharaja's palace above Rishikesh — and, at the same time, a serious medical wellness centre. A doctor writes your week after a consultation on arrival. Nothing is compulsory: some guests do six hours of therapy a day, some read by the pool.

5 nights · A restorative pause

Long enough to stop, not long enough to change much. Best placed after three nights in Delhi and Agra, before the rest of the journey.

7 nights · The one we suggest most

The shortest genuinely therapeutic stay — rejuvenation, sleep or stress programmes begin to work, and the doctor can adjust mid-stay. Rishikesh and the Ganges aarti on the way out.

10 nights · Measurable change

What weight, pain and fitness programmes need to shift something you can measure. Then Rajasthan afterwards, unhurried.

14 nights · Panchakarma territory

The classical staged cleanse, which cannot honestly be compressed. Keep the rest of the trip gentle and fly home from Delhi.

Where to put it in the route

At the beginning, to arrive over the jet lag and start the journey rested — or at the very end, so India finishes quietly rather than at an airport.

Combinations That Work

Six ways our travellers put them together.

— 10 nights

Golden Triangle & Ranthambore

Delhi, the Taj at dawn and Jaipur, then three tiger drives from a tented camp at the Ranthambore gate. The gentlest way to add a forest to a first trip.

— 16 nights

Ananda, then Rajasthan

Seven nights of therapy in the Himalayan foothills first, then Jaipur, Jodhpur and Udaipur at half the usual speed — because by then you will want it that way.

— 14 nights

Kerala & Kabini

Cochin, a kettuvallam houseboat through the backwaters and the Munnar tea hills, then elephant herds and the black panther on the Kabini reservoir.

— 18 nights

Tigers, the Taj & a Quiet Week

Bandhavgarh and Kanha for the forests, Agra for the Taj Mahal, and a closing week at Ananda so the journey ends rested instead of exhausted.

— 12 nights

Lions & the West

Mumbai's island city and Elephanta, the Gir forest for wild Asiatic lions, then Udaipur's lake palaces to finish. Best December to April.

— 15 nights

Rhino & the Ganges

Kolkata, four nights at Kaziranga for the one-horned rhino, then Varanasi for the evening aarti and Delhi for the flight home.
